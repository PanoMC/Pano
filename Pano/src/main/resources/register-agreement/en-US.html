Registration Agreement

By creating an account you agree to the rules below. You can be warned, muted, kicked or banned if you break them.

1. Your account

  • You are responsible for everything done with your account. Keep your password to yourself.
  • Use a username that is not offensive and does not pretend to be someone else.
  • Do not buy, sell or share accounts.

2. Behaviour

  • Be respectful. Insults, harassment and hate speech are not allowed.
  • Discrimination based on race, gender, religion or any other personal trait is not allowed.
  • Do not spam the chat, the forums or private messages.

3. Cheating and exploits

  • Hacked clients, cheats and unfair modifications are not allowed.
  • Report any bug or exploit you find to the staff instead of using it.
  • Duplicating items, abusing glitches and similar unfair methods are not allowed.

4. Advertising

  • Do not advertise other servers or services.
  • Do not share harmful or inappropriate links.

5. Staff decisions

  • Follow the instructions of the staff. Their decisions are final.
  • These rules can change. The current version always applies.
